# Orbit Hopper: Store Listing

## Title
Orbit Hopper

## Short description (~80 characters)
Tap to launch, hop between planets, and chase the stars!

## Full description
Orbit Hopper is a one-tap arcade game about timing and nerve.

You circle a glowing planet. Tap at the right moment to blast off in a straight line, and catch the orbit of the next planet before you drift into the void. Aim for the center of an orbit to land a Perfect capture and earn double points, and grab the golden stars floating between worlds for bonus points.

The farther you go, the trickier it gets: orbits shrink, speeds climb, and some planets start to drift from side to side. How many worlds can you hop across?

How to play
- Tap anywhere (or press Space) to launch off your current planet.
- Fly into a planet's orbit ring to lock on and start circling it.
- Miss every planet and the run is over.

Features
- Simple one-tap controls, easy to learn and hard to put down
- Perfect captures and golden stars for big scores
- Rising difficulty with moving planets
- Your best score is saved automatically
- Relaxing synth sound effects with sound toggle
